Operate your infrastructure from your phone — without fighting the keyboard.

Fewshell is an AI-augmented mobile SSH client built with strict operational safety and security guardrails for real-world production use. Typing complex shell commands on a phone is slow and error-prone. Instead, steer AI to do most of the heavy lifting for you. Fewshell generates the commands, you review, approve, edit, and execute safely.

(( BUILT FOR ON-CALL REALITY ))

AI-AUGMENTED WORKFLOW
Stop typing long commands on a tiny keyboard. Ask for what you need. The AI drafts commands — you stay in control.

NO YOLO MODE
Every command requires your approval. There is no way to turn this off. No accidents or surprises.

SECURE BY DEFAULT
End-to-end encrypted connections. Secrets stored only in your device’s secure keychain. Automatic redaction keeps credentials out of logs and AI context.

COMPLETE SESSION HISTORY
Every interaction is stored on your device and your server so you know exactly what changed and when.
